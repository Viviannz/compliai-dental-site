Questions

What counts as a message?

Each reply the screener gives you counts as one message. A full assessment usually runs across a few messages, because the screener works in stages: the compliance table first, then the summary and priority actions, then a vendor letter if you need one.

As a rough guide, 100 messages a month covers around 25 to 30 full screens.

What happens when I reach my monthly limit?

The screener pauses and lets you know. Your allowance refreshes each month, so you can pick up again once it resets.

Can I paste patient information into the screener?

No. Please describe scenarios using fictional or fully anonymised details. The screener assesses tools, workflows, scripts and content. It does not need, and should not receive, real patient data.

Is this legal advice?

No. CompliAI Dental is a screening tool that helps you spot regulatory risk and see the next safe step. Where an issue needs a specialist, the verdict says so and tells you what to check. See the full terms and disclaimer.

I run a practice with several clinicians. Is there a team plan?

Each plan is currently for one user. If you would like access for a whole team, email hello@dentalamplify.com and we will talk it through.

Stop guessing on compliance

Most AI compliance tools are generic, bloated, or just static PDF checklists. They do not understand dentistry. The instruments differ by country, but the domains that catch dental practices out are the same everywhere:

  • Data protection · biometric consent, patient data residency and cross-border transfers
  • AI transparency · risk tiers and disclosure obligations, led by the EU AI Act
  • Professional standards · clinical claims, advertising and registrant accountability
  • Medical device rules · the point at which clinical AI becomes a regulated device

Screened in depth against UK and EU instruments including UK GDPR, GDPR, the EU AI Act and MDR 2017/745, with divergence flagged where local rules differ.

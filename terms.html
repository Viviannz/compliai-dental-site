Disclaimer

This tool is a compliance screening instrument. It is not legal advice and must not be treated as such.

CompliAI Dental is an AI-powered compliance screening tool designed to help dental professionals and healthcare technology businesses identify potential regulatory issues in AI systems, proposals, scripts, and workflows before seeking qualified legal or regulatory counsel.

What CompliAI Dental does

  • Provides a preliminary regulatory screen against five frameworks: UK GDPR / EU GDPR, the EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689), GDC Advertising and Communication Standards, the Medical Device Regulations (MDR 2017/745), and ICO AI Guidance.
  • Returns structured verdicts (PERMITTED / DISCLOSE REQUIRED / PROHIBITED / SEEK SPECIALIST ADVICE) with references to specific articles or guidelines.
  • Identifies issues in AI proposals, agent scripts, business plans, and product descriptions submitted by the user.

What CompliAI Dental does not do

  • Provide legal advice or act as a solicitor, barrister, or regulatory consultant.
  • Assess vendor Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) or confirm their legal adequacy.
  • Register systems with Eudamed, the ICO, or any regulatory authority.
  • Make GDC fitness-to-practise determinations.
  • Guarantee that a system assessed as PERMITTED will be found compliant by a regulator.
  • Substitute for independent legal review before deployment of any AI system.

Verdict key

All CompliAI Dental assessments use the following four verdict labels. Understanding these labels is part of using the tool correctly.

Permitted
The element described passes the compliance screen as described. No specific regulatory action is required before proceeding. Standard good practice (data minimisation, security) still applies.
Disclose required
The element is permitted only if specific disclosure, consent, or labelling steps are taken first. The assessment will specify exactly what is required.
Prohibited
The element as described is not permitted under the current regulatory framework. It must be redesigned before deployment. The assessment will specify the governing rule.
Seek specialist advice
Compliance status depends on factors this tool cannot assess, such as a specific vendor's DPA terms, MDR classification details, or jurisdiction-specific rules. Independent specialist advice is required before proceeding.

A PERMITTED verdict means the element passed this screening tool's nine-rule check. It does not constitute regulatory clearance, legal sign-off, or a guarantee of compliance. Always verify with qualified legal counsel before deployment.

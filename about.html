Why this exists

I'm Dr Vivian Onu-Nzegbulem. I have practised dentistry for nearly three decades, and I now build AI systems for dental teams.

The pattern I kept seeing was simple. Dentists are being offered AI tools faster than anyone can check them: receptionist bots, note-takers, chatbots, AI-generated videos. Every one of them touches patient data, professional standards or advertising rules, and the questions that matter rarely come up in the sales demo.

So I built the screener I wanted for my own work. Before I deploy a workflow or publish a video, I run it through the same ten rules this site uses. It does not replace a solicitor or a regulator. It helps you see the risk early and know exactly what to ask next.

03

What the screener checks

Paste a scenario, workflow, script, advert or vendor proposal. The screener tests it against ten rule areas and returns a structured verdict for each element, with the rule triggered and the action needed.

  • Patient data, health data and biometrics
  • AI disclosure and synthetic media transparency
  • Dental advertising and professional conduct
  • Data residency, international transfers and sub-processors
  • Medical device risk
  • AI risk classification
  • Privacy governance and clinical oversight
  • Automated decisions and human intervention
  • Provider and deployer accountability
  • AI account tier and contract coverage

Each finding is marked permitted, disclose required, prohibited, or seek specialist advice. The verdict key explains each one.

04

What it is not

dentalcompliance.app is a screening tool, not legal advice. It does not approve, certify or register anything. Where an answer depends on facts it cannot verify, such as a vendor's contract terms or medical device status, it says so and tells you what to check.
